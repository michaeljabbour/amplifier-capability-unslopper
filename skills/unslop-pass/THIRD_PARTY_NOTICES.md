# Stop-Slop lineage

The prose review policy adapts mechanisms from Michael J. Jabbour's Stop-Slop v0.5.0 in
[amplifier-capability-research](https://github.com/michaeljabbour/amplifier-capability-research/tree/b91be99dd8ebbebb0736c469764b60eba97df2e1),
specifically `behaviors/stop-slop.md` and `context/prose-quality/{phrases,structures,examples}.md`.
That work credits [Hardik Pandya's Stop-Slop](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop).
The adaptation retains both copyright notices and their MIT terms below. The source's blanket
style bans and claim-strengthening examples are not adopted. Research papers are cited, not copied.
