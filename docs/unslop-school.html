Every AI design starts from the same defaults

Models reach for the same fonts, the same gradients and the same little labels because those choices were safe in their training data. This course teaches you to name what you see, spot the defaults, fix them, and then give a design a direction of its own.

Learn the vocabulary

You cannot direct a model, or a designer, toward something you cannot name. These twenty terms cover type, layout, color and motion. Each one comes with what it means and why it shows up when you work with AI.

2

Spot the slop

Slop is what happens when every choice is a default. None of these twenty patterns is wrong in itself. Each one becomes a tell when it appears without a reason, and when it appears alongside four others.

They are grouped by where they live, so you can scan a screen one layer at a time.

Notice that dithering, ASCII art and gradients also appear in lesson 6 as ways to make a design richer. That is the whole lesson in one contradiction. A technique is slop when the model chose it for you, and craft when you chose it for the subject.

What changed

  1. Section numbering and eyebrow. "02 · PLAN" restates the headline and implies an order the reader does not need. Removed.
  2. Filler pill. "New" says nothing about the feature. Removed.
  3. Periods, random italics, Instrument Serif, tight kerning. Replaced by one specific sentence in a face chosen for the product.
  4. All-caps subtitle. Three adjectives nobody reads. Removed.
  5. Code breadcrumb. Mono used as decoration. Mono now appears only inside the real plan, where it is code.
  6. Verbose copy. Thirty words became twenty, with the outcome stated plainly.
  7. Two equal ghost buttons on a gradient. One solid call to action, one quiet secondary link, on a calm ground. The plan itself is shown as the product.

Twenty ways to fix it

The fixes fall into four moves. Most of the work is subtraction. The rest is choosing one thing to make important, then building a small system so the rest stays consistent.

Give it a direction with three colors

Removing slop gets you to clean. Clean is still forgettable. The fastest way past it is to commit to a world: a dark ground, a light, and one signal color, each pulled from the subject itself. Type and imagery then follow the palette.

These three use italics, wide-tracked caps and serif display faces, which lesson 2 flags. They work because every choice belongs to the brand's world and repeats with discipline. That is the test to apply to any rule in this course.

Step 1

Name the feeling

One sentence about a place or moment. "Aurora over a watch dial." Avoid adjectives like modern or clean.

Step 2

Pick three roles

Ground, light, signal. The signal color covers under 10 percent of the screen.

Step 3

Let type follow

Choose a face the subject would use on its own signage, packaging or poster.

Step 4

One hero image

An image that carries the idea and uses the palette. The product, ideally.
